# Related Work Notes — Intelligence & Graph Architecture (Riya)

## 1. Indian Corporate Registry & Director Interlocks ("Who Sits Where")
- Corporate registries can be modeled as bipartite or projected graphs connecting directors (DIN) to firm entities (CIN).
- Structural network analysis surfaces dense director interlocks, corporate clusters, and central hub nodes that link seemingly independent companies.
- High graph density combined with shared geographic attributes (addresses/pincodes) strongly correlates with coordinated corporate structures.

## 2. Existing Commercial Fraud & Anomaly Detection Systems
- **Prophecy Eagle I:** Entity resolution and visual link analysis platform for tracking multi-layered company relationships in public registries.
- **Innefu Labs (Shell Company Detection):** Network-based anomaly detection using AI and graph analytics to flag circular ownership and shell company clusters.
- **Accumn Fraud Detection:** Risk scoring engine leveraging unsupervised machine learning to detect structural deviations in registry metadata.
